Questions

Quick answers before you install.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start tracking. There is no sign-up.

Why do I have to allow installation from outside the Play Store?

The app is distributed as a direct APK download, so Android asks you to confirm the install. You can turn the permission off again once the app is installed.

Do I pay anything extra to use it?

No. The app is free, with no ads, no subscription and no booking fee. You pay only the normal daily local fare for your ride.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Where is Ride is available for Android only.

How do I get updates?

Download the latest APK from this page and install it over the existing app.
